# Clinical Policy CP-RAD-014: MRI of the Lumbar Spine (Prior Authorization)

*Fictional policy written for demonstration. Not clinical guidance.*

## 1. Urgent indications

MRI of the lumbar spine is approved without further review when the request includes a diagnosis of cauda equina syndrome (G83.4), suspected vertebral metastasis (C79.51), or vertebral osteomyelitis (M46.2). Urgent indications take precedence over all other criteria in this policy.

## 2. Pediatric requests

Requests for members under 18 years of age are pended for review by a pediatric radiologist.

## 3. Repeat imaging

A request is denied when the member had a lumbar MRI within the past 12 months, unless the request documents a new neurological deficit. Repeat imaging criteria are evaluated before routine criteria.

## 4. Routine indications

For non-urgent low back pain, MRI is approved when the member is 18 or older, has completed at least 6 weeks of conservative therapy, and has had symptoms for at least 6 weeks.

Requests where the member has completed fewer than 6 weeks of conservative therapy are denied.

## 5. Network

Requests from out-of-network ordering providers are pended for network review.

## 6. Default

Requests not addressed by this policy are pended for clinical review.
